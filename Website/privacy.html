Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Developer: Igor Chernakov

Kestrel Frontier does not collect personal data or send gameplay analytics to the developer. The app has no account system, advertising SDKs, or tracking.

Information on your device

The app keeps settings and campaign progress locally on your device. The developer does not receive or have access to that information. Removing the app may remove its locally stored data, subject to your device’s backup and storage settings.

Local-network multiplayer

If you choose local multiplayer, the app uses your local network to discover nearby sessions and exchange live game state with participating devices. This includes the network addresses needed for the connection and session information displayed in the game browser. This traffic is used for the game session; it is not sent to a developer-operated server or used for tracking.

You can decline or revoke local-network permission in your device’s Settings. Solo campaign play does not require that permission.

No advertising or tracking

The app does not use advertising identifiers, behavioral advertising, third-party analytics, or cross-app tracking. It does not request access to contacts, precise location, the camera, or the microphone.

Support and this website

If you voluntarily post a support request on GitHub, GitHub processes the information you submit and your post is public. Please do not post personal or sensitive information. The developer uses support requests to investigate and respond to problems.

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP addresses and request logs, when you visit. This site adds no analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its practices.

Children’s privacy

The app does not collect personal information from children or other players. Content age ratings are displayed separately on the App Store.

Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the app’s data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current version.

For privacy questions, contact Igor Chernakov through the project’s support channel. Keep requests general and do not include private information in public posts.
