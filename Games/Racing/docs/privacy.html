Privacy Policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Arcline: Neon Racing is developed by Igor Chernakov. The game has no account sign-in, advertising, tracking, or analytics. The developer does not collect player data through the game.

On your device

Arcline saves your driver name, garage, credits, race progress, and preferences on your device so you can continue playing. Use Settings → Reset Career to reset your career. Deleting the app removes its locally stored data from that installation.

Local multiplayer

If you choose to host or join a race, Arcline requests Local Network access. Devices in the session exchange information such as your driver name, car setup, and race state over the local network to display and run the race. Arcline does not send this information to the developer, advertising services, or analytics providers. You can turn off Local Network access for Arcline in your device settings and continue racing solo.

Support

If you open a public support request, GitHub handles it under its privacy statement. Visit Arcline Support for help or questions about this policy.
