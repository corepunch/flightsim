Arcline Support

Find help with racing, your garage, and local multiplayer in Arcline: Neon Racing for iPhone and iPad.

Start your first race

Tap Race, switch to Circuit, and choose an event. All races are free to enter, with fixed prizes for qualifying finishes. Losing never costs credits or a car. Steer with the left stick. On touch, A accelerates, B brakes and X pulls the handbrake. On a paired controller, use the right trigger for gas, the left trigger for braking and the bottom face button for the handbrake. Hold the regular brake fully at a standstill to reverse. Pull the handbrake as you turn to slide the rear out, then release it to regain grip. You can adjust driver assist and haptics in Settings.

Race on the same Wi-Fi

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network. Open Online and host a race on one device. On the other device, select the room to join. If the room is missing, check that Arcline has Local Network access on both devices.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, Arcline version, and what you were doing when the issue appeared. Reports are public. Leave out personal information.
