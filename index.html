Help for Kestrel Frontier, a story-driven space combat game for iPhone and iPad.

Contact support

Support is provided by developer Igor Chernakov through GitHub Issues. A free GitHub account is required to post.

Support requests are public. Do not include passwords, purchase receipts, payment details, or other private information.

Starting the campaign

Choose the campaign from the main menu, select an available mission, read the briefing, and launch. Campaign play runs on your device and does not require an account.

Controls and display

Play in landscape orientation using the on-screen flight controls. A compatible game controller is optional. The game includes cockpit and chase views.

Local Wi-Fi dogfights

Connect both devices to the same Wi-Fi network. One player hosts a game; the other joins through the local server browser. Allow local-network access when iOS asks.

If a host does not appear, check the app’s Local Network permission in Settings and confirm the Wi-Fi network allows devices to communicate. Guest networks may isolate devices.

Report a problem

Include your device model, iOS or iPadOS version, app version, mission name, and the steps that led to the problem. Explain what you expected and what happened. Screenshots can help; remove private information first.

About the game

Fly a 20-mission campaign from the carrier CNS Bastion. Defend convoys, rescue survivors, and target the shields, engines, and reactors of enemy capital ships.
