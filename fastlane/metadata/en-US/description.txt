Up to 8 pilots. One Wi-Fi network. Nonstop space combat.

Kestrel Frontier turns any room into a starfighter arena. Pick up a phone, a tablet or a laptop, find the game on your network and you're in the fight within seconds: no internet, no logins, no waiting for matchmaking. And when the room goes quiet, a 20-mission story campaign is ready for you, with new chapters in the works.

PLAY WITH WHOEVER'S AROUND
Parents versus kids after dinner. Cousins over the holidays. Design against engineering on a Friday afternoon. A computer lab after the last bell. If they're on the same Wi-Fi, they can fly.

ONE BATTLE, EVERY DEVICE
iPhone, iPad and Mac share the same battlefield. One player hosts, everyone else taps Join, and anyone who arrives late drops straight into the action.

MAKE YOUR OWN RULES
Choose the fighter everyone flies, how many pilots can join, the kills needed to win, how long the round lasts and how fast the fallen return. A quick skirmish or an hour-long showdown: your call.

SIMPLE TO START, SATISFYING TO MASTER
Casual controls steer like a game: push sideways and the nose turns, so first-timers are dogfighting in moments. Expert controls fly like a real aircraft: bank, then pull into the turn. Use touch controls or a game controller.

A STORY WORTH FLYING SOLO
From the decks of the carrier CNS Bastion, a routine patrol pulls your squadron into a conspiracy that threatens the gates linking human worlds. Twenty handcrafted missions of convoy escorts, rescues, carrier defense and deep strikes, told through briefings, tactical maps and debriefs. More missions are on the way.

TAKE DOWN GIANTS
Corvettes, destroyers, cruisers and a towering dreadnought stand in your way. Pick apart their shields, engines, turrets and reactors to bring them down.
