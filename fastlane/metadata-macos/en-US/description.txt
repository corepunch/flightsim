Take flight on the Kestrel frontier. A routine border patrol draws your squadron into a conspiracy that threatens the gates connecting human worlds. From the carrier CNS Bastion, fly with your wingmen, protect the people caught in the conflict, and uncover the truth behind a manufactured invasion.

A CAMPAIGN ACROSS 20 MISSIONS
Patrol remote navigation routes, escort vulnerable freighters, rescue survivors, defend your carrier, and strike enemy installations. Follow a connected story through character briefings, tactical maps, and mission debriefings.

FIGHT FROM THE COCKPIT
Pilot a space fighter in 3D combat with lasers and torpedoes. Manage your approach, track hostile ships, and fight alongside your squadron. Switch between cockpit and chase views.

TAKE ON CAPITAL SHIPS
Face corvettes, destroyers, cruisers, and a massive dreadnought. Target shield generators, engines, turrets, and reactors to break through their defenses.

LOCAL WI-FI DOGFIGHTS
Host or join a match with friends on the same local network. Campaign missions can be played solo without an account.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Fly with keyboard, mouse, trackball, or a compatible game controller. The game plays well with a trackball: relative steering, adjustable horizontal and vertical sensitivity, pitch inversion, and Casual or Expert controls help you find your preferred feel.
